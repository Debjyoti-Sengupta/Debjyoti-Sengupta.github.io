Bio

Debjyoti Sengupta is a final year Ph.D. student at Missouri University of Science and Technology who started in the Fall of 2022. He has a B.Tech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the West Bengal University of Technology in 2014. He has done his M.Tech in Mechatronics from the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology, Shibpur in 2016. He was working at Hyundai Motor India Ltd. from 2016 to 2022. Currently, he is doing is Ph.D. in Computer Science under the supervision of Dr. Sajal Das at Missouri University of Science and Technology. His research interests include Robotics , Intelligent Systems and Autonomy in general. His current research is in Urban Air Mobility (UAM).
